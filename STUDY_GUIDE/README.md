# Study Guide

Short guides to read on your own, before or after the matching day. Each day has a quick guide that explains every topic in simple English with an analogy, a tiny example and a short recap. Two longer MCP papers sit next to them for extra reading.

## Quick guides, one for each day

| Guide | What it covers | Read after |
|---|---|---|
| [Day 1: Prompting, structured output and cost](DAY_1_STUDY_GUIDE.md) | One call to Claude, choosing a model, prompts that hold up, structured output and checks, cost (count, cache, batch) | Day 1 labs |
| [Day 2: Tools and MCP](DAY_2_STUDY_GUIDE.md) | The tool-use loop, tool design, errors and retries, parallel calls, MCP basics, MCP security | Day 2 labs |
| [Day 3: Agents](DAY_3_STUDY_GUIDE.md) | Workflow or agent, multi-agent design, task decomposition, safety and failure handling, building agents with the SDKs | Day 3 labs |
| [Day 4: Claude Code](DAY_4_STUDY_GUIDE.md) | Plan Mode, CLAUDE.md and rules, skills, hooks and subagents, MCP and context, the CI review gate | Day 4 labs |
| [Day 5: Memory, guardrails and evals](DAY_5_STUDY_GUIDE.md) | Context and memory, three kinds of agent, guardrails by layer, errors and escalation, evals and provenance, the capstone | Day 5 labs |

Each quick guide ends with a "Go deeper" list that links to the full guides in `../STUDY_GUIDES/`.
