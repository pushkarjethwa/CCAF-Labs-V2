# Feature request: birthday bonus

From the Brew & Bean marketing team.

Members should get a small thank-you on their birthday. When a member makes a purchase on the day of their birthday, they earn 50 extra points on top of the normal points for that purchase.

Notes from the team:

- The bonus is a whole number of points, like all points.
- It applies to members only.
- The service must not log the birthday or any other personal data. Log the customer id only.
- The request will carry the member's birthday as a field, for example `birthday` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form.
- Like every change to `src/`, it needs tests.
